When to audit a free claim

One actuator serves several decaying explorers on a ring. Explorers report a noisy urgency for free, hidden shocks make health hard to predict, and every dispatch costs travel. This project measures what exact state information is worth and what happens when explorers report strategically, including when a dispatcher should check claims against the truth.

Two demos let you watch the mechanisms play out: dispatch under reported urgency (Demo 1) and when to audit a claim (Demo 2). The papers are working drafts and have not been peer reviewed.
